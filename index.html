Jujutsu rewards conditions.

Mechanics

THE SYSTEM

Core mechanics, presented as a field note rather than a box-filled encyclopedia.

POWER IS
CONTEXT.

Cursed Energy

Negative emotion produces cursed energy; control, reserves, output and efficiency are different variables.

Innate Technique

A characteristic ability with its own operating rules, applications and limits.

Binding Vow

A restriction can be exchanged for an advantage. The terms are the power.

Domain Expansion

A manifested Domain can impose a sure-hit effect under the rules of a completed lethal Domain.

Heavenly Restriction

A severe innate trade-off can produce exceptional physical or other advantages.

Black Flash

A tiny timing window between impact and cursed energy creates a spatial distortion and heightened state.

The trick is not knowing every rule.

It is noticing which rule a fighter is exploiting. Range, timing, vows, barriers and information can matter more than raw reserves.
