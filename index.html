Cursed energy leaks from every human heart — fear, hatred, regret. Left to rot, it congeals into Curses: things born from us, shaped by us, hunting us.

Jujutsu sorcerers are the only ones who can see them, and the only ones who can exorcise them. It is a rotten profession. Someone still has to do it.
