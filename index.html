THE SYSTEM

Power is a language.

Cursed energy is not a generic “mana bar.” Technique, restriction, vows and domains all change what a sorcerer can do—and what they must pay for it.

Jujutsu mechanics

Everything has a price.

Every technique sits inside a web of conditions: control, output, innate ability, binding vows, heavenly restriction, anti-domain measures and the terrifying finality of a complete Domain Expansion.
