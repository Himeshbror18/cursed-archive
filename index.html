Cursed Energy 呪力

Negative energy born from human negative emotions — fear, grief, hatred, regret. It flows through the body like blood. Sorcerers learn to control, reinforce, and expel it. Output determines raw power; efficiency determines how long you can fight.

Cursed Techniques 呪術式

Innate abilities engraved in a sorcerer's body and soul, unique to each individual. Advanced applications are Technique Extensions — like Gojo's Blue, Red, and Hollow Purple.

Binding Vows 縛り

Self-imposed rules that trade a restriction for a power boost. Sukuna and Yuji's binding vow involved the trigger “Enchain” and a one-minute takeover condition; the terms were deliberately concealed from Yuji.

界

Barrier Techniques

Constructs of cursed energy that create spaces — from veils to Domain barriers. Shibuya was trapped behind massive veils.

具

Cursed Tools

Weapons imbued with cursed energy. Toji's Inverted Spear of Heaven nullified any technique on contact.

天

Heavenly Restriction

A vow imposed at birth. Toji and Maki gained superhuman bodies with zero cursed energy — invisible to sorcerers.

黒

Black Flash 黒閃

The Grade System

Sorcerers ranked Grade 4 to Special Grade. Special Grade sorcerers could single-handedly topple nations.

心

Negative Emotion

Fear, grief, hatred, regret — the fuel of all cursed energy. Cursed spirits arise from accumulated cursed energy associated with negative human emotions; the relationship is broader than a simple one-emotion-equals-one-power rule.

魂

The Soul

Techniques are engraved in the soul itself. Mahito's Idle Transfiguration reshapes souls — and bodies — at will.

域

Domain Expansion

A high-level barrier technique that manifests the user's innate domain and cursed technique. Completed lethal Domains can carry a sure-hit effect; not every Domain is complete or constructed the same way. See below.

How Domain Expansion Works

1. The Barrier

Many Domains use a barrier to define the space and rules, but barrier construction varies. Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine is the notable open-barrier exception.

2. The Innate Technique

The user's innate technique is imbued into the Domain, producing its characteristic environment and effect.

3. The Guaranteed Hit

A completed lethal Domain can impose a sure-hit effect. Incomplete Domains and non-lethal rule-based Domains do not all work identically.

4. The Hand Sign

Distinctive hand signs are part of the activation choreography for many Domains, but their complexity is not a measure of power. The signs vary by user and can be one- or two-handed.

5. Domain Clash

When Domains clash, their barriers and sure-hit effects interact; refinement, conditions, and barrier mechanics determine the outcome. It is not simply a generic 'stronger attack wins' system.

6. Technique Burnout

Domain Expansion can leave the user's innate technique in burnout afterward, although characters can use cursed-energy control and other techniques to manage or recover from the drawback.

Reference · Hand Seals

Hand Signs of Domain Expansion

Many Domain users perform distinctive hand seals, but not every Domain has a neatly documented one, and a seal's complexity does not determine the Domain's strength.
