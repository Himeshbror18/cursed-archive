01 · THE RULES

What actually governs jujutsu?

A compact ruleset for reading the world without reducing it to “bigger number wins.”

Cursed Energy

Negative emotion becomes fuel.

Cursed energy is produced from negative emotions. Sorcerers learn to control its flow, reinforce their bodies, and apply it to techniques. Output, efficiency, reserves, and control are different variables.

Innate Technique

The technique is the user's system.

An innate cursed technique gives a sorcerer a characteristic method of manipulating cursed energy. Skill is not just raw output: interpretation, conditions, timing and efficiency matter.

Binding Vow

Rules can buy power.

A binding vow trades a restriction or risk for an effect. The exact terms matter. Breaking a vow can carry consequences, but “vow = free power” is not how the system works.

Domain Expansion

A technique becomes a territory.

A Domain manifests an innate Domain through barrier techniques and imbues it with a cursed technique. Completed lethal Domains commonly have a sure-hit effect; incomplete and non-lethal Domains can operate differently.

Anti-Domain

There are counters.

Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Falling Blossom Emotion and other techniques can interfere with Domain sure-hit effects. Escaping the effective area or attacking the caster can also matter.

Heavenly Restriction

Less can become more.

A Heavenly Restriction imposes a binding condition on the body, sometimes trading cursed energy for extraordinary physical ability or other advantages. Toji and Maki demonstrate different consequences of this system.

02 · SEASON 2

The fracture

Two arcs, one structural break: Gojo's past explains the relationship that later becomes the key to sealing him.

2006–2007 · HIDDEN INVENTORY / PREMATURE DEATH

Gojo and Geto break apart.

Young Satoru Gojo and Suguru Geto escort Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel. Toji Fushiguro's intervention and Riko's death push Gojo toward a new level of mastery while Geto's worldview begins to collapse.

OCTOBER 31 · SHIBUYA INCIDENT

The strongest is removed.

The curse users' operation is built around one objective: isolate and seal Satoru Gojo with the Prison Realm. Once he is removed, the balance of the jujutsu world changes immediately.

AFTERMATH

The old system starts eating itself.

With Gojo sealed, the higher-ups issue orders against Yuji, Yuta is brought back into the picture, and Kenjaku's plan moves from a hidden operation toward a system-wide crisis.

03 · CURRENT CANON STATUS

Where JJK actually stands now

This is the part the old “Season 2” section could not explain without becoming obsolete.

Main series

Jujutsu Kaisen is finished.

The original manga concluded in September 2024, with volume 30 being the final volume. So there is no ongoing main-series cliffhanger to “wait for.”

Modulo

A separate future story exists.

Jujutsu Kaisen ≡ (Modulo) is a short future-set spin-off written by Gege Akutami and illustrated by Yuji Iwasaki. It takes place 68 years after the Culling Game and follows a new era of sorcerers and an extraterrestrial encounter.

The useful cliffhanger

What remains unresolved depends on which story you mean.

For the original manga, the central narrative has an ending. For the wider JJK setting, the interesting open questions are legacy, the changed jujutsu system, and what the future generation inherits. Modulo explores that future rather than leaving the original finale unfinished.

Anime status

Season 2 is a historical checkpoint.

Season 2 covers Hidden Inventory / Premature Death and the Shibuya Incident. Its ending is deliberately catastrophic, but it is not the current state of the manga canon.
