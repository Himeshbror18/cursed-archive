The Rules

What actually governs jujutsu?

Enough mechanics to understand why the fights work without turning the archive into a spreadsheet.

Cursed Energy

Negative emotion becomes fuel.

Cursed energy is generated from negative emotions. Sorcerers control it to reinforce the body and activate cursed techniques.

Innate Techniques

Technique conditions matter.

Innate techniques have individual rules, limitations and applications. Output and efficiency are not the same thing.

Binding Vows

Restrictions can create advantages.

A vow trades a condition, limitation or risk for an effect. The exact terms matter more than the dramatic label.

Domain Expansion

Territory + technique.

A Domain manifests an innate Domain using barrier techniques and embeds the user's cursed technique. Completed lethal Domains commonly carry a sure-hit effect.

Anti-Domain

There are counters.

Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket and Falling Blossom Emotion can interfere with Domain sure-hit effects under their respective conditions.

Heavenly Restriction

Trade-offs can be extreme.

Severe innate restrictions can produce exceptional physical ability or other advantages; Toji and Maki are major examples.

02
Season 2

The fracture

Hidden Inventory explains the broken relationship. Shibuya weaponizes it.

2006–2007 · HIDDEN INVENTORY / PREMATURE DEATH

Gojo and Geto stop seeing the world the same way.

Gojo and Geto protect Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel. Toji Fushiguro kills Riko and nearly kills Gojo; Gojo survives, awakens to a new level of control, and Geto begins a much darker ideological descent.

OCTOBER 31 · SHIBUYA INCIDENT

Gojo is sealed.

The curse users construct the situation around one objective: trap Satoru Gojo long enough for the Prison Realm to activate. Once Gojo is removed, the balance of the jujutsu world collapses.

AFTERMATH

The crisis becomes systemic.

Yuji is again marked for execution, Yuta is brought back into the conflict, and Kenjaku's plan moves toward the Culling Game and a fundamental reshaping of jujutsu society.

Current status · September 2026

Where JJK actually stands

The old page treated Season 2 like “current events.” That is no longer accurate.

The original Jujutsu Kaisen manga ended with chapter 271 in September 2024; Shueisha lists volume 30 as the completed series. The short sequel Jujutsu Kaisen ≡ (Modulo) also ended with chapter 25 in March 2026. There is therefore no unfinished main-series cliffhanger waiting for a new weekly chapter.
Anime

Season 3 finished its scheduled first part.

The official anime site states that “Culling Game Part 1” ran through episode 59, broadcast March 26, 2026. Season 2 is now a retrospective chapter of the story, not the current anime endpoint.

Modulo

The sequel also has an ending.

Modulo began in September 2025 and concluded with chapter 25 in March 2026. Its setting is 68 years after the Culling Game, in 2086, with the Okkotsu descendants and the Simurians.

The real cliffhanger

It is thematic, not a missing chapter.

The original ending leaves the wider world with questions about the future of jujutsu, the legacy of its strongest figures, and what a changed generation does with the rules it inherited.
