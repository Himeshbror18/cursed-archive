01
Seven rules

The Jujutsu System, actually explained

These are the seven core mechanics worth knowing before a fight starts getting complicated. They are an archive framework, not an official numbered “seven rules” list.

01

Cursed Energy

Negative emotions generate cursed energy. Sorcerers learn to control its flow for reinforcement, techniques and other jujutsu applications. Amount, output, efficiency and control are different qualities.

Fuel · reinforcement · output
02

Innate Cursed Techniques

An innate technique is a characteristic ability engraved into the user. Its applications depend on the technique's own rules, the user's interpretation and their level of control.

Technique · conditions · mastery
03

Reverse Cursed Technique

Reverse Cursed Technique converts cursed energy into positive energy. It is difficult to perform and is used for high-level healing and certain technique applications.

Positive energy · healing
04

Binding Vows

A binding vow imposes a restriction or condition in exchange for an advantage. The bargain only makes sense when its terms are specific; the restriction is part of the power.

Restriction · exchange · risk
05

Barrier Arts & Domains

Barrier techniques manipulate territory and information. Domain Expansion manifests an innate Domain, with completed lethal Domains commonly carrying a sure-hit effect.

Veils · barriers · sure-hit
06

Anti-Domain Techniques

Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket and Falling Blossom Emotion can counter or mitigate Domain sure-hit effects under their particular conditions. A Domain is not automatically checkmate.

Counter · timing · conditions
07

Heavenly Restriction

A condition imposed on a person from birth can trade away one aspect for another extreme advantage. Toji and Maki are the clearest examples of the physical end of this exchange.

Trade-off · body · limitation
08

Black Flash · bonus mechanic

Black Flash is intentionally kept outside the seven-rule primer: cursed energy applied within an extremely small timing window after a physical hit produces a spatial distortion and heightened combat state.

Timing · distortion · insight
02
Season 2

How the fracture happens

Season 2 is two stories that become one disaster: the making of Gojo and the breaking of Geto, followed by the operation designed to remove Gojo from the board.

2006–2007 · HIDDEN INVENTORY / PREMATURE DEATH

Gojo and Geto begin as inseparable partners.

They protect Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel. Toji Fushiguro intervenes, kills Riko and pushes Gojo to the edge of death. Gojo awakens to a new level of cursed-technique mastery; Geto's worldview begins to collapse under the repeated cruelty of jujutsu society.

THE BREAK

Geto chooses ideology over the system.

The gap between their conclusions becomes irreconcilable. Gojo becomes the strongest within the existing system; Geto decides the system itself is the problem.

OCTOBER 31 · SHIBUYA INCIDENT

One objective: seal Satoru Gojo.

The curtain, crowd control and layered curses are designed around one strategic problem: make Gojo spend enough time in a chaotic environment for the Prison Realm to activate. Once he is removed, the balance of modern jujutsu collapses.

AFTERMATH

The personal disaster becomes a systemic crisis.

Yuji's execution order returns, Yuta re-enters the conflict, and Kenjaku's plan moves toward the Culling Game. From this point forward, the story is no longer just about defeating individual curses; it is about the structure of jujutsu society itself.

Current canon

And what about the manga?

The archive separates three different things people often mix together: Season 2, the completed original manga, and the short sequel.

Original manga

Jujutsu Kaisen ended with chapter 271.

The main series concluded in September 2024. So any “cliffhanger” you see today is an anime adaptation endpoint or a lingering world question, not an unfinished original serialization.

Jujutsu Kaisen ≡ (Modulo)

The sequel also concluded.

Modulo finished with chapter 25 in 2026. It jumps 68 years beyond the Culling Game to a 2086 setting involving descendants of the Okkotsu family and the Simurians.

The real open question

What does the next era do with jujutsu?

The largest remaining questions are thematic: how a new generation handles inherited rules, what survives from the old jujutsu order, and what a changed world does with the power structures left behind.

Archive policy

Canon and interpretation stay separate.

Dates, episode endpoints and mechanics are treated as factual records. Predictions and thematic readings are labelled as interpretations instead of quietly being promoted into “canon.”
